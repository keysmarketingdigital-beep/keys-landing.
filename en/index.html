// FAQ

What people ask us most.

Can I see something before deciding?

Yes. You can open our prototypes and try them yourself in the examples section. After the first meeting we show you how yours would look.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. In the first meeting, which is free, we work out the numbers with you and give you a clear price before starting, with no hidden costs.

How long does it take?

Setup takes 10 business days once the scope is defined. Before starting we always show you a prototype you can see and try, so you decide with confidence.

Do I have to change my WhatsApp or my tools?

No. We work with what you already use, like your WhatsApp number and your social media, and connect the new tools to them.

Who owns what you build?

Yours. Your domain, your accounts and your data stay in your name, and each business keeps its data separate from the rest.

What happens after delivery?

We teach you to use it, answer your questions and keep refining it with you. We don’t leave you alone with the tool.
