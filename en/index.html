01Website

With your website

02Online booking

Every appointment you book by chat takes time away from work.

Confirming, moving and reminding appointments over WhatsApp eats up hours and invites mistakes: two clients at the same time, someone who doesn't show, midnight messages nobody answers. With online booking, clients see open slots and book themselves; you just confirm.

Today, over WhatsApp

  • You answer messages while you're with a client.
  • Double bookings and forgotten appointments.
  • When you're closed, nobody can book.

With online booking

  • Clients book at any hour, even at night.
  • Slots block themselves: no two appointments overlap.
  • Reminders and each client's history.
See what booking includes

Each tool comes with its own landing page and three logins: one for whoever books, one for each team member and one for the admin.

The client

Books at any hour, changes or cancels without calling.

  • Sees open slots
  • Picks service and professional
  • Gets a confirmation

Each team member

Barber, dentist, vet or stylist: sees only their own day and clients.

The admin

Controls hours, team and services in one place.

  • Whole-team schedule and new bookings
  • Services, prices, promotions and blocked times
  • Monthly balance and commissions per person

Which businesses

Clinics, vets, barbershops, salons and spas: the same tool with each business's brand and services.

03Accounting and audit

If nobody reviews your numbers, problems show up when it's too late.

Many businesses know how much they sell, but not how much they keep or whether they're over- or under-paying taxes. And accounting errors don't warn you: they surface at year-end or in a notice from the tax authority, when it's too late to fix them. An audit reviews what already happened, flags the errors and tells you what can still be fixed in time. At KEYS it's done by an accountant specialized in tax management, not just by software.

Today, with no review

  • You know what comes in, not what's left.
  • You find an error when the tax notice arrives.
  • You sign tax returns you can't check.

With an accounting audit

  • Books reviewed and up to date.
  • Errors surface before the tax notice does.
  • You decide with real figures in hand.
